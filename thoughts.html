Statement of Purpose. I think a lot, but recently I've been thinking about the depth of my day-to-day thoughts. A lot of my thoughts, especially due to short-form media consumption, are spoon fed to me and not thought through. So, my goal here is to explore and grow my thinking with a practice that is so low pressure, but will help me articulate my thoughts. I want to use this website journal to keep myself accountable with daily to weekly entries. I already have so many topics I’m hoping to write about—mainly political topics surrounding technology, education, and philosophy. I also want to outline health and wellness goals here. Many of these topics will be influenced by current events, classes I take, books I read, and largely, my personal experiences. Anything important to me will be noted at some point, so bear with me if the writing is cheeks to begin with because that’s the whole point! Oct 7, 2025.
