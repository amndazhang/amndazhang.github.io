A short update. I had totally forgotten to update this page in months, and it's kinda funny the first post here was on October 7th. I'm back because I'm trying to reject cringe and practice writing more. It's currently the day before my first final exam of Fall 2025, and I wanted to give an update on what's been up. I recently saw someone talk about how these life updates you often give to people when catching up are quite performative of who you WANT to be since you're an unreliable narrator of your life. Anyway, I've been working on inconviencing myself more, despite how stupid that sounds. I mean the idea has gained a lot of popularity online, and it's all about honoring your body and mind over convinient desires. For me, this has looked like sitting myself on my patio to meditate for 5 minutes every morning, planning out my daily food intake and sticking to it, and getting to bed by midnight. I definitely have struggled a lot to stick to these all, especially food since there are so many temptations trying to distract you from eating a balanced diet, especially during finals season. I also want to integrate daily journaling, daily reading, listen to music intentionally, routinely work on my hobbies, and definitely more! I've also recently been enlightened on the corruption of the university system, and practically most large systems in place in the global north. Key is definitely creating genuine connections with people around, fostering community through mutual aid, and kind of letting go of uncontrollable worries. It's crazy how much clarity and release I feel from writing my thoughts down, even if it's shit, I need to do this more often. I'll be back soon for a semester reflection. December 14, 2025. Statement of Purpose. I think a lot, but recently I've been thinking about the depth of my day-to-day thoughts. A lot of my thoughts, especially due to short-form media consumption, are spoon fed to me and not thought through. So, my goal here is to explore and grow my thinking with a practice that is so low pressure, but will help me articulate my thoughts. I want to use this website journal to keep myself accountable with daily to weekly entries. I already have so many topics I’m hoping to write about—mainly political topics surrounding technology, education, and philosophy. I also want to outline health and wellness goals here. Many of these topics will be influenced by current events, classes I take, books I read, and largely, my personal experiences. Anything important to me will be noted at some point, so bear with me if the writing is cheeks to begin with because that’s the whole point! Also just tryna take up more space and put my words out there, even if this isn't going to be read. October 7, 2025.
